## Top Stories

### NU7 Vote Results Are In

The NU7 coinholder poll and ZCAP poll both closed September 14. Participation hit a record: 2.4M ZEC voted out of 3.6M in the Ironwood pool — 66%. The outcomes:

- **Q1 — Issuance:** Preserve halvings (coinholders 98.9%). ZCAP was nearly split: 57 smooth vs. 54 preserve.
- **Q2 — NSM Reissuance:** February 2031 (coinholders 96.6%). ZCAP and panels voted ASAP. This was the biggest divergence.
- **Q3 — Sprout:** Disable v4 transactions immediately at NU7.
- **Q4 — Block Times:** Yes to 25-second blocks (ZIP-218).
- **Q5 — Scope:** Ship NU7 by September 30, dropping any feature not ready.

The Q2 divergence prompted discussion — coinholders overwhelmingly chose 2031 while panels voted for immediate reissuance. Shielded Labs, Tachyon, ZODL, ZF, and Valar Group have aligned on February 2031 as the most conservative interpretation, noting they can revisit with another poll if circumstances change. Forum discussion also noted that subtracting 2.3M ZEC from the winning side would only flip Q2 and Q3 — raising familiar questions about whale influence in coinholder voting. [Results thread](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/nu7-sentiment-polls-results/57590) · [Coinholder vote thread](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/nu7-coinholder-vote/56912) · [ZecBlock governance tracker](https://zecblock.com/governance/nu7)

## Protocol & Development

### zcashd: One Node Left

The original Zcash client — the one that launched the network in October 2016 — is down to a single active node on mainnet. Last edition counted 4; the edition before that, still a handful. Zebra (463 nodes, 81%) and Zakura (93 nodes, 16%) now run the network. This is the end of an era, happening quietly.

### Zakura Ships the First Formally Verified zk-SNARK Prover

On September 12, Sean Bowe announced that Zakura now has the first and only formally verified zk-SNARK prover — a machine-checked Lean proof of statistical honest-verifier zero-knowledge, built by Tachyon's @MariusMargulus and integrated into both Zakura and Vizor. This augments the soundness proofs published in July. The practical implication: Zcash's prover correctness is no longer just peer-reviewed, it's mathematically machine-checked.

### ZF Engineering: Zebra Sync Fixed

The Zcash Foundation's engineering update covers the last two weeks: Zebra can now sync reliably to the chain tip after recurring problems since the NU6.3 upgrade. They also replaced Mergify with GitHub's native merge queue, simplifying CI. [Update](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zf-engineering-update-24-august-6-september-2026/57511)

### Hashrate Continues Climbing

Network hashrate reached 30.01 GSol/s, up 8.8% from last week's 27.58. This is consistent with the new mining capacity that came online in late August.

## Privacy Corner: When the Price Runs, the Chain Talks

ZEC's 2x run since July has done something measurable to on-chain behavior. Transaction volume went from a steady 4,500/day to sustained 8,000–15,000/day. Shielded percentage has held above 44% every day for two weeks.

But not all volume is equal. Some of it is new participants. Some is profit-taking — shield, move to exchange, sell. Some is exchanges reshuffling custody wallets. Network Pulse flagged 13 anomalies the first week and has since calmed to 3, suggesting the initial shock has been absorbed.

The useful signal isn't the volume itself — it's the shielded ratio. If a price run brought mostly transparent activity (exchange shuffles), that would suggest the chain is being used as a speculative asset. The fact that shielded activity kept pace suggests at least some of the new users are actually using the privacy features. Whether they stick around after the excitement fades is the real test.
