## OkTop Stories

### NU7 Is Confirmed: Testnet October 6, Mainnet November 5

All Zcash development orgs — Shielded Labs, Tachyon, Valar Group, ZODL, and the Zcash Foundation — reached unanimous agreement on the NU7 timeline and contents. The upgrade includes 25-second block spacing (ZIP-218), disabling v4 transactions, and NSM integration (ZIP-234/235) with halvings preserved and reissuance starting February 2031. New transaction formats are not in this upgrade. Sean Bowe called it the most aligned the ecosystem has been: "NU7 is coming!" [NU7 Timeline](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/nu7-timeline/57655) · [ZecBlock governance tracker](https://zecblock.com/governance/nu7)

### Shielded Ledger Support Is Live

Vizor shipped shielded ZEC support for Ledger hardware wallets — desktop and mobile. This has been in the works since April 2025. Hardware wallet users can now send and receive shielded Zcash without giving up custody. [Ledger thread](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/a-path-forward-for-ledger-and-zcash/50951)

### Q3 Retroactive Grants: 37 Proposals, Vote Is Live

The largest retroactive grants round to date opened September 17. 37 proposals from teams that built first and applied for funding after. The coinholder poll closes September 29 at 20:00 UTC. Vote in Vizor. [Voting thread](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/q3-coinholder-directed-retroactive-grants-program-voting-now-open/57650) · [Proposals](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/30-day-review-period-coinholder-directed-retroactive-grants-program-q3/57056)

## Privacy Corner: $550M in Cross-Chain Volume

Cross-chain ZEC volume crossed $550M all-time this week — up from $422M just two weeks ago. That's $128M in cross-chain flows in 14 days, the fastest growth rate we've tracked.

The 24-hour numbers tell the story: $12.7M in volume across 1,228 swaps. All-time swaps passed 83,000. This isn't a single spike — it's sustained daily volume at levels that didn't exist before the ETF listing and the price run.

What's notable is that this volume is flowing through decentralized bridges, not just centralized exchanges. Users are moving ZEC across chains, which means the privacy features matter — you don't bridge to a privacy chain unless you intend to use the privacy.
