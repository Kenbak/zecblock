## Top Stories

### Trustless Shielded ZEC to EVM Atomic Swaps — No New Signing Primitives

A team demonstrated fully trustless atomic swaps between shielded Zcash and Ethereum, without requiring new signing primitives. The breakthrough leverages Zcash's Segregated Witness scheme (v5 ZIP-244) and works despite Zcash having no scripting. The thread drew 22 posts and immediate interest — a follow-up explored whether the 52-byte context window is expressive enough for cross-chain programmability while keeping the sender anonymous. If this holds up, it's a meaningful step toward shielded DeFi without bridges. [Thread](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/we-just-did-a-fully-trustless-shielded-zec-to-evm-atomic-swap-with-no-new-signing-primitives-and-yes-it-is-programmable/57827) · [Cross-chain programmability follow-up](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/shielded-to-evm-cross-chain-programmability-while-keeping-the-sender-anonymous/57828)

### 38,000 New ZEC Holders on Solana in Six Weeks

Ben, CMO at Raydium, posted about the rapid growth of ZEC distribution on Solana — 38,000 new holders in six weeks. The thread (20 posts, 477 views, 52 likes) is the most-discussed topic of the week. The community discussion explores what wrapped ZEC on Solana means for privacy, whether these holders will discover shielded Zcash, and what the ecosystem should do about it. [Thread](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/38-000-new-zec-holders-on-solana-in-six-weeks-now-what/57788)

### Zebra 6.4.0 and 6.4.1 Recalled — Upgrade to 6.4.2 Immediately

A high-severity bug in Zebra 6.4.0 and 6.4.1 allowed any peer to crash a node with a single network message — no handshake or authentication required. Both versions are recalled. Zebra 6.4.2 fixes the vulnerability and also includes the sync stall fix from 6.4.1. Zebra 6.3.0 and earlier are not affected. All operators should upgrade immediately. [Security advisory](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zebra-6-4-2-emergency-fix-for-a-remotely-triggerable-crash/57872) · [Original release thread](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zebra-6-4-0-and-6-4-1-security-fixes-light-client-serving-and-nu7-preparation-updated/57789)

## Privacy Corner: The Quiet Exit from Transparent

Something has shifted this month. Migration transaction count went from 62,256 three weeks ago to 92,756 today — 30,500 new migration transactions. But the Ironwood pool hasn't grown proportionally. It's been hovering just under 4M ZEC for two weeks.

What's happening? The flows are more balanced now. Early migration was one-directional: Orchard and Sapling into Ironwood. Now, people are also spending from Ironwood — buying things, moving to exchanges, swapping cross-chain. The pool is absorbing new funds and also being used as money. That's the transition from "migration" to "usage."

Meanwhile, the transparent pool dropped from 12.0M to 11.9M ZEC. Slow, but the direction hasn't reversed. Every week, a little more ZEC moves behind the curtain.
