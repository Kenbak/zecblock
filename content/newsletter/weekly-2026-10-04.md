### NU7 Activates on Testnet — 25-Second Blocks Are Here

NU7, the next major Zcash network upgrade, activated on public testnet at block 4,465,026 on October 4. The headline feature: blocks now come every 25 seconds instead of 75. That cuts first-confirmation wait from over a minute to under half a minute — a real difference at point-of-sale, on exchanges, and for cross-chain bridges. NU7 also introduces a Network Sustainability Mechanism that recycles part of every transaction fee into future block rewards, plus new shielded limits to protect wallets from spam. Daily ZEC issuance stays the same. Mainnet operators don't need to do anything yet — no mainnet activation height is set. [Zebra 7.0.0-rc.0 announcement](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zebra-7-0-0-rc-0-nu7-arrives-on-testnet/58006)

### Q3 Retroactive Grants: 37 Projects Funded

The Q3 Coinholder-Directed Retroactive Grants Program closed September 29, and the results are in: 37 projects received funding. The funded list spans security research (bug bounties for the Orchard counterfeiting vulnerability and five critical Zebra consensus divergence bugs), infrastructure (Zec.rocks, lightwalletd-rs, CipherScan), protocol work (Ironwood zk-SNARK formal verification, ZODL core development), wallets (Unstoppable Wallet, Zafu, Nozy, Zallet), tooling (ZecKit, ZecBooks, Zcash Names), and community efforts. Q4 call for proposals opens October 30. [Full results](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/q3-coinholder-directed-retroactive-grants-program-results/57909) · [Analysis: "The Hand of God"](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/the-hand-of-god-what-q3s-coinholder-retroactive-grants-vote-tells-us/57910)

### NEAR Intents Hacked — $3.8M Stolen

NEAR's custodial contracts on Omni were hacked, with attackers stealing $3.8M. This matters for Zcash because ZODL's cross-chain integration uses NEAR Intents. The thread discusses the implications and what this means for shielded cross-chain infrastructure. A timely reminder that bridges remain the weakest link in cross-chain design. [Forum](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/near-intents-was-hacked-yesterday/57978)

## Protocol & Development

Zebra 7.0.0-rc.0 shipped — see Top Stories for NU7 details. Key NU7 changes:

- **25-second blocks** (ZIP 218) — 3x faster confirmations, same daily ZEC issuance
- **Network Sustainability Mechanism** — transaction fee recycling into future block rewards
- **New shielded limits** — wallet-level spam protection

Testnet operators should upgrade before activation. Mainnet operators should ensure they're running Zebra 6.4.2 (last week's emergency fix) but don't need to act on NU7 yet.
