## Top Stories

### Gem Wallet Adds Native ZEC Swaps via THORChain

Gem Wallet now supports native ZEC swaps in-app, powered by THORChain's recently added Zcash support. No wrapped ZEC, no bridge, no exchange account — swap directly between ZEC and other THORChain-supported assets. This is another step in ZEC becoming natively tradeable across ecosystems without custodians. [Forum](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/gem-wallet-now-supports-native-zec-swaps-via-thorchain/58093) · [THORChain blog post](https://blog.thorchain.org/bringing-native-zcash-to-thorchain)

### Zcash Turns 10 This Month

October 28, 2016: the Zcash genesis block was mined. This month marks the 10th anniversary. The community is celebrating — a dedicated "[Zcash anniversary month](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-anniversary-month/58053)" thread is collecting events, retrospectives, and milestones. Zcon7 in Cancún (Oct 27–29) lands right on the birthday weekend.

### ZF Ships Four Zebra Releases in Two Weeks

The Zcash Foundation's engineering update covers September 21 through October 5 and it's dense: Zebra 6.4.0 and 6.4.1 brought security fixes and light wallet serving, 6.4.2 was an emergency fix for a remotely triggerable crash, and 7.0.0-rc.0 is the first NU7 release candidate. Specific security fixes: tampered block handling, peer ban evasion via IPv6 rotation, and 24-hour ban expiry. NU7 activated on testnet at block 4,465,026 on October 4. [Engineering update](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zf-engineering-update-21-september-5-october-2026/58059)

### ZF Hires a New Technical Program Manager

Joan De Arcayne joins the Zcash Foundation as Technical Program Manager. Background in crypto product launches and engineering, focused on keeping the distance between ideas and working code as short as possible. [Forum](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-foundation-welcomes-a-new-technical-program-manager/58071)

## Protocol & Development

### NU7 Testnet: First Week

NU7 has been running on testnet since October 4. Blocks come every 25 seconds. Testnet operators who haven't upgraded to Zebra 7.0.0-rc.0 are off the NU7 chain. The Foundation is monitoring for issues before a mainnet activation date is finalized.

### Grant Pipeline Growing

The volume of grant applications this week — from React Native wallets to Solana privacy tooling to Korean-language research — signals growing developer interest. Notable: multiple applications target native shielded integration, not just transparent ZEC wrapping. The ecosystem is building toward privacy as the default path, not an afterthought.

## Privacy Corner: Ten Years of Private Money

In October 2016, Zcash launched with a radical proposition: money should be private by default. Ten years later, the transparent pool is shrinking, shielded transactions are climbing, and the Ironwood pool just crossed 4M ZEC.

The ecosystem has grown in ways nobody predicted — trustless cross-chain atomic swaps, shielded DeFi research, native THORChain support, and hardware wallet integration for shielded spending. The math that seemed academic a decade ago now protects real money for real people.

The 10th anniversary isn't just a milestone. It's evidence that privacy-preserving money isn't a niche experiment — it's infrastructure.
