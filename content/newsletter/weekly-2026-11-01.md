## Top Stories

### The Dev Fund Fight: A Week of Competing Proposals

ZEC's price rise has made Zcash Community Grants far larger than it was designed to be. Sean Bowe puts its funding at over $125M. Within a week, that set off the biggest governance debate in years. In order:

- **Oct 26: End ZCG's stream.** Maxime Desalle [proposed](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/end-zcg-and-send-its-funds-to-the-nsm/58515) ending ZCG at NU8, redirecting its 8% to the Network Sustainability Mechanism (NSM) and sending its leftover treasury there too. His argument is that coinholders fund ZCG but don't choose it. His thread drew 365 likes and 141 posts. Sean Bowe filed a [matching draft ZIP](https://github.com/zcash/zips/pull/1414) the same day.
- **Oct 27: End the whole Dev Fund.** Josh Swihart [replied](https://x.com/jswihart/status/2114943225937232107) that ending only ZCG is a half-measure, since the 12% coinholder stream has the same problem. He later [proposed ending the entire Dev Fund](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/time-to-end-the-zcash-development-fund/58610) before its November 2028 expiry.
- **Oct 27: Soft landing.** ZecHub's dismad [drafted](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/draft-zip-dev-fund-soft-landing/58519) a schedule that halves both streams at each halving and reaches zero after the fifth one (~2036).
- **Oct 29: ZCG's own taper.** ZCG [floated](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcg-gets-too-much-zec-heres-our-plan-to-fix-that/58566) cutting its intake to about 47,000 ZEC by 2028, down from about 109,000, and returning the rest to the NSM. It has since withdrawn the idea, saying the decision belongs to the community's governance process. A separate [wind-down ZIP](https://github.com/zcash/zips/pull/1423) was also filed.
- **Oct 30: Future Dev and Legal Defense Fund.** Sean Bowe (Tachyon), Zooko and Jason McGee (Shielded Labs) [proposed](https://github.com/zcash/zips/pull/1428) ending ZCG's stream and sending the same 8% to a new lockbox for future development and legal defense until the third halving. ZCG would also donate 50,000 ZEC to the NSM by sending it to an unspendable address that is credited to the NSM at activation. Zooko [asked](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/future-dev-and-legal-defense-fund-zip/58611) other proposers to withdraw theirs and back it. Bowe's October 26 draft was closed the same day.
- **Oct 31: Zcash Adoption Pool (ZAP).** Daira-Emma Hopwood and others wrote a [ZIP at Zcon7](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zip-zcash-adoption-pool-zap/58656) defending grants for broad ZEC adoption, as ZIP 1015 intended. It drew 557 likes and 283 posts, the most-engaged thread of the week.

No proposal is final. Any change needs a network upgrade after NU7.

### Zcash Turns 10; Zcon7 in Cancún

The genesis block was mined on October 28, 2016. Zooko [marked the day](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/happy-birthday-zcashers-one-and-all/58561) by asking people to thank a Zcash supporter, developer or coinholder. He also noted that almost all of the original core team still works on Zcash full-time. Zcon7 ran October 27–29 in Cancún, and some attendees [cancelled late over safety concerns](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/reflections-on-zcon-the-community-forum/58654). Two of the week's biggest outputs came from the conference: the ZAP draft above and Zebra Ándale below.

### Zebra Ándale: A Performance Release for Miners

Built from miner feedback at Zcon7, [Zebra Ándale](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zebra-andale-the-fastest-zebra-yet/58643) (7.1.0-alpha.0) targets the slow paths that matter more under 25-second blocks: building block templates, accepting solved blocks and relaying them. In ZF's own benchmark, it accepted a prepared mixed block in 17 ms. ZF says that is 9.6× faster than Zebra 7.0.0 and 3–4× faster than Zakura 1.7.0. It is an alpha for testing, not the NU7 release. Operators still need Zebra 7.0.0 or Zakura 1.7.0 before activation.

### Q4 Coinholder Retroactive Grants Open

The Financial Privacy Foundation [opened the Q4 round](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/q4-2026-coinholder-directed-retroactive-grant-program-call-for-proposals/58598) for already-completed work. Proposals are due November 13 at 20:00 UTC, with review November 16 – December 16 and a coinholder vote December 17–29. Security researchers filed first. Applications cover a [Critical Zebra block-discovery DoS](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/retroactive-grant-application-critical-zebra-block-discovery-dos-cve-2026-44499/58657) ($150K requested), a [High-severity mempool DoS](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/retroactive-grant-application-zebra-high-severity-mempool-dos-disclosure-ghsa-7jp4-mq53-4cvq/58600) fixed in Zebra 7.0.0 ($75K), and a [High-severity consensus bug](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/retroactive-grant-application-zebra-high-severity-consensus-vulnerability-cve-2026-40880/58723). [Maya Protocol](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/retroactive-grant-application-maya-protocol-zcash-upstream-contributions-nu6-2-nu6-3-ironwood-zebra-migration-and-zec-signing-hardening/58622) and [HoudiniSwap](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/houdiniswap-retroactive-grant-application/58507) applied too.

## Privacy Corner: Crossing a Pool Boundary Is Public

On November 1, a single transaction moved **9,093.61 ZEC** from Sprout to Sapling ([`c0174bcd…7e1a`](https://zecblock.com/tx/c0174bcd6bbd798a88ed73d96b94e9089fd05d1c2d17b71a3a1979e5ecfc7e1a)). Alert bots reported it as a "shield". It wasn't: the transaction has no transparent inputs. Its Sprout value balance is +9,093.61 ZEC and its Sapling value balance is −9,093.61 ZEC. It stayed shielded the whole time.

It still leaked something. Every pool keeps a public balance so the network can check that no ZEC is created out of thin air. So when value crosses from one shielded pool to another, the amount crossing is public, even though sender and recipient stay hidden. Anyone watching now knows that someone held over 9,000 ZEC in Sprout and moved it on November 1.

[ZIP 308](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0308), the Sprout-to-Sapling migration design, handles this deliberately. It moves funds over time in a series of transactions with varied amounts, so no single crossing stands out.

**Action:** Sprout funds become unspendable when NU7 activates (block 3,543,405, around November 5), so move them now, or they are lost. For Sapling there is no deadline yet. When you do move, use your wallet's migration tool or split the move into uneven amounts over several days.
