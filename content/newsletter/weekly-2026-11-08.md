## Corrections

Last week's Privacy Corner said Sprout funds not moved before NU7 "are lost." That was too strong. NU7 makes the Sprout funds unspendable under current consensus rules, but they [have not been burned](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/57718/46). No wallet can spend them today, and any way of recovering them would need a future consensus change.

## Top Stories

### NU7 Is Live: 25-Second Blocks and the Network Sustainability Mechanism

NU7 activated at block [3,543,405](https://zecblock.com/block/3543405) on November 6 at 02:34:02 UTC ([announcement](https://x.com/Zcash/status/2118534331090284580)). Target block spacing fell from 75 seconds to 25, and the block subsidy was divided by three, so daily issuance stays the same. The first 9,819 NU7 blocks averaged 25.5 seconds through the end of November 8. On November 8 alone, 3,453 blocks arrived, or one every 25.02 seconds.

From now on, 60% of each block's fees go to the Network Sustainability Mechanism (NSM) reserve, to be reissued through future block rewards from [around 2031](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/42703/335). The reserve held 369.47 ZEC at the close of November 6 and 371.23 ZEC on November 9.

ZecBlock's node recorded 24 one-block reorgs in those 9,819 blocks, and none went deeper. Orphan rates depend on how many competing blocks an observer catches: zkDragon [reports](https://x.com/zkDragon/status/2119435630828728710) 0.89% after the upgrade against 0.66% before.

### Sprout Freezes With 12,224 ZEC Inside

Sprout spends require version 4 transactions, and NU7 made those invalid. The pool's balance has held at 12,224.21 ZEC since activation. It held about 22,591 ZEC [at the start of September](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/57718/46), and most of the drop came from the single 9,094 ZEC migration on November 1. [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/11/05/zcash-is-about-to-freeze-usd16-million-in-its-oldest-privacy-pool) valued the frozen balance at about $16 million.

### ZCG and the Dev Fund: A Poll Opens November 16

Project Tachyon, Shielded Labs, Valar Group, the Zcash Foundation and ZODL, the five organizations that coordinate the coinholder voting chain, [published a timeline](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcg-and-the-dev-fund-timeline-for-proposals-and-polling/58906) for deciding what happens to ZCG's share of the Dev Fund. Proposals closed on November 8 at 23:59 UTC. Draft poll questions come out November 9 for a week of public comment. Polls for coinholders and community panels run November 16–30. If rough consensus emerges, engineering teams and miners would ship the result in a small upgrade called NU7.1.

The arguments kept coming. Paul Brigner [argued](https://x.com/paulbrigner/status/2117291473750175987) for balanced governance and investment in adoption. T. S. Felstead [opposed](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/the-zcg-lockbox-one-tap-off-the-bigger-one-left-running/59027) both the lockbox proposal and ZAP as written, and wants both streams to expire in 2028 as scheduled. iJaack [filed a draft ZIP](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/coinholder-grants-need-to-fund-ecosystem-work/58764) so coinholder grants cover business development, developer relations and education, after finding that 93.1% of approved Q3 money went to security and core infrastructure.

How the vote itself should work is now its own debate. Arya's [draft ZIP](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/draft-zip-lock-weighted-coinholder-polling/58813) would weight votes by how long coins are locked after the snapshot. Zec.rocks [plans a parallel December poll](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/introducing-coinperson-voting-one-vote-per-coinholder/58768) that tries to give each coinholder one vote.

## Privacy Corner: The Account They Didn't Know About

A barista named Sarah got a call at work. The caller knew her name, her workplace, her boss and her Square account, said her boss had been arrested, and talked her into emptying the account. Square couldn't help, and neither could her bank. [As Zooko told it](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/sarahs-story-from-losing-her-savings-to-23-79-zec-in-shielded-tips/58873), one thing the scammers didn't know about was the Zcash she had saved from years of tips. She sold some for the first time, paid her rent and refilled her bank account.

We usually talk about privacy as protection from governments and corporations. Sarah's case is smaller and more common. Every detail the scammers used was a piece of her life that some system had exposed, and each one made the lie more believable. They took what they could see. What they couldn't see, they couldn't ask for.

That is the security argument for financial privacy, and it has nothing to do with having something to hide. A balance nobody can look up can't be used to pick you as a target, or to work out how much to ask for.

**Action:** Keep some of your savings somewhere no one can look up. Shield it, and don't tell anyone how much is there.
